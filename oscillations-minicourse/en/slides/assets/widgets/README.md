# Interactive widgets (English)

The mini-course keeps no widget code of its own. The scripts and stylesheets live in
`introduction/ru/slides/assets/` (the modules carry a `{ru,en}` dictionary and pick English
from the `lang` of the page), and the includes here are generated from the English fragments
`_*.en.qmd` that sit next to them:

```sh
python3 scripts/sync_minicourse_widgets.py
```

Kept here by hand:

- `_matter_constant_lab.qmd`, `_matter_adiabatic_lab.qmd` — the compact laboratories of the
  mini-course. Their script, `06_matter_oscillations/matter_oscillations.js`, is the shared one
  (`../../_metadata.yml` loads it for every deck); `matter_oscillations.css` is the reduced
  stylesheet of these labs (the `:root` colors and the "Interactive laboratories" section).
- `_energy_phase_animation.qmd` — inline CSS and script, no shared source.
- `_scales_lab.qmd` — two panels on one distance axis: the three-flavor survival probability of ν̄e for one
  source at distance L and for all sources of the Earth closer than L (one energy or the geoneutrino IBD
  spectrum); below, the signal from sources closer than L, emitted by the crust and the mantle (from their density
  and U, Th content) and detected as ν̄e;
  inline CSS and script, NuFIT 6.1 normal ordering. Its spectrum `geo_ibd_spectrum.js` is generated by
  `../oscillations/geo_spectrum.py` from S. Enomoto's U and Th chain spectra (not stored in the repository).
  The energy slider covers the geoneutrino interval 1.8–3.3 MeV only.
- `_atm_lab.qmd` — an atmospheric νμ: the vacuum three-flavor probabilities νμ → νμ, ντ, νe against the distance
  L(θz) from the birth at h ≈ 15 km (Gaisser & Stanev 1998) to the detector, for one energy (0.5–100 GeV) and one
  zenith angle, no averaging; inline CSS and script, NuFIT 6.1 normal ordering (checked against `osc.py`).
  It also draws the detector, the zenith angle and the path over the cut Earth `earth-cut-atm.png` on the slide
  (`<svg id="atm-earth">`), with the projection from `earth_cut_atm.js` (generated by `../oscillations/earth_cutaway.py`).
- `_birefringent_setup.qmd`, `_birefringent_lab.qmd` — light between a polarizer H and an analyzer V through a calcite
  plate of thickness L with axes at θ: the field along the beam (animated), the polarization ellipse behind the plate,
  the intensities against L and the readouts; labels typeset by MathJax; `birefringent.{js,css}`. Calcite indices at
  589.3 nm from the dispersion formulas of G. Ghosh, Opt. Commun. 163, 95 (1999). `_two_waves_light.qmd` is no longer used.
- `_osc_problem.qmd` — the problem of the lecture as an experiment: a source makes a neutrino with e⁺, a detector at L
  (slider or drag) makes e⁻ or μ⁻; 150 events per second drawn with P(νe → νμ) = sin²2θ sin²(πL/L_osc), θ = θ12 (NuFIT 6.1),
  counted and binned in L; the picture stays calm (a running dashed beam, two bars with the e⁻ and μ⁻ shares at this L,
  counters refreshed five times per second); the curve on request; switches m1 = m2 and θ = 0; `osc_problem.{js,css}`.
- `_solar_twist.qmd` — a νe born at the center of the Sun, followed to the surface (two flavors, adiabatic): the axes ν1m, ν2m
  turn with θm(r), the shares of ν1m and ν2m stay at cos²θm0, sin²θm0, the νe share is ½ + ½ cos2θm0 cos2θm(r); at high
  energy ν2 leaves the Sun. Energy slider and the Borexino energies (pp 0.267, ⁷Be 0.862, pep 1.44, ⁸B 8.1 MeV);
  BS05(OP) n_e/N_A = 102.9 cm⁻³ at the center, e^(−10.54 r/R☉); NuFIT 6.1; `solar_twist.{js,css}`.
- `_two_waves_vacuum.qmd`, `_two_waves_light.qmd`, `_two_waves_matter.qmd` — two waves leaving the source in phase,
  their relative phase at a movable detector and the probability along the path; `two_waves.{js,css}`.
- `_crystal_matrix.qmd` — the neutrino crystal E(1 − n) in the basis ν1, ν2, in units of Δm²/2E: vacuum diag(0, 1)
  plus A times the projector on νe gives a turned crystal; readouts θm, θm − θ, sin²2θm and the new index difference R;
  the axes ν1, ν2, ν1m, ν2m and νe, νμ in one plane; labels typeset by MathJax; `crystal_matrix.{js,css}`.
  Solar center: n_e/N_A = 102.9 cm⁻³ (BS05(OP)), 10 MeV, A = 2.08 (also in `two_waves.js`).
  Big numbers: diag(0, 1) + A·P_e = the crystal; the button "Diagonalize" adds diag(λ1, λ2), λ1,2 = (1 + A ∓ R)/2, and turns
  the picture from the angle θ (νe to ν1) to the new axes ν1m, ν2m and θm. The formulas live on the slide (tabs Phases, Propagation).
- `_nu_crystal.qmd` — the neutrino crystal drawn like the calcite plate (`nu_crystal.js`, styles from `birefringent.css`):
  source νe, a layer of vacuum or matter of length L with the axes ν1, ν2 (dashed) and ν1m, ν2m, the flavor state as a
  Jones vector, detector νμ; sliders A and L/L_osc; P(νe → νμ) against L in vacuum and in the medium.
